# Survey question map

## RQ1 — Current review practice

- Information normally used, up to three choices.
- Information checked first.
- Reasons for waiting for more review, up to two choices.
- Which error is usually more costly.
- Whether current software explains doubtful detections.
- Reasons for questioning peak-marking software.
- One optional missing-information comment.

## RQ2 — Effects of separate evidence

For each of the same three cases:

- Accept, Reject, or Defer.
- Confidence, 0–100.
- Most influential clue.
- Response time.
- Whether the decision changes from signal only to separate evidence.

After each condition:

- Helpfulness.
- Clarity.
- Mental effort.
- Ability to judge when to trust the software.

## RQ3 — Conflicting evidence and confusion

For evidence-bearing conditions:

- Whether clues pointed in different directions.
- Main reason the case was hard to judge on selected trials.
- Clarity of the uncertainty information.
- Hardest clue after each evidence condition.
- Whether the amount of information was too little, about right, or too much.

## RQ4 — Effect of an explicit AI peak mark

In the final condition, the participant sees:

> AI DECISION: PEAK. ACCEPT AS A PEAK.

The study records:

- Participant decision.
- Whether the participant followed the AI peak mark.
- How strongly the AI statement influenced decisions.
- Whether it changed an answer the participant would otherwise give.
- Decision change from separate evidence to AI advice on the exact same case.
- Four derived reliance categories.

## End-of-study answer review

After all nine decisions, participants see the study comparison label and their three decisions for each shared case. The labels are hidden until the end to avoid influencing later views.
