# Case-bank notes

The demonstration bank contains 48 cases derived from synthetic and operationally representative signals.

## v3 assignment behavior

- Three unique cases are selected per participant.
- The same three cases are repeated in all three conditions.
- Case slots remain fixed: Case 1 matches Case 1 across views.
- Obvious categories beginning with `clear_` are excluded from participant assignments.
- Hidden case metadata is used to prioritize ambiguous cases.
- Participants never see hidden reference status, hidden case category, or conflict labels during the decision task.

## AI recommendation condition

The participant-facing recommendation is intentionally constant and explicit:

> AI DECISION: PEAK. The AI is explicitly marking this candidate as a peak and recommends accepting it.

The original case-bank recommendation remains researcher metadata and is hidden from participant views.
